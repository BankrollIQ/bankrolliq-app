My bets & Game Watch Beta

Save a bet you already placed. We show the price the other side needs for you to lock in a profit whichever team wins, and — with Game Watch on — check live prices every minute during the game and email you when your target is available. Two-way moneylines only.

Game Watch checks once a minute from an hour before the game until it ends, using sportsbooks and exchanges (including Kalshi and Polymarket where listed). After it emails you, it stops for that bet — turn it back on to keep watching. Prices move fast and books can pause betting; always confirm before placing a hedge.

$500 daily target — feasibility

Two separate checks of whether today's candidates could reach $500 combined worst-case profit. The verified plan uses only opportunities whose fees, terms and wager limits were confirmed, sized within those limits. The theoretical plan also uses unverified candidates and is a calculation, not a plan to act on. Each dollar is counted once, and positions where a push or tie returns $0 are left out.
